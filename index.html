Gestures

  • Works with your left hand, right hand, or both at once
  • One finger up: plant flowers
  • Two fingers up: move the ring over a button and hold to press it
  • Three fingers up: erase
  • Open palm, swipe right: next slide. Swipe left: previous
  • Fist: pause
  • Keyboard: arrows to move, C to clear, F for fullscreen
